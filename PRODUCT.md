# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

General public who need a quick QR code for links, WiFi passwords, or contact cards, without signup or installation.

## Product Purpose

Create and download QR codes instantly in the browser, with customization (colors, style, logo, caption) and multiple export formats (PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF).

## Positioning

Free, browser-only QR generator with no accounts or tracking; exports are generated client-side and include optional captions and brand styling.

## Operating Context

Used on desktop and mobile browsers; static site hosted on GitHub Pages; no backend or persistent server.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- QR types: link/text, WiFi, vCard
- Customization: color, background, style, logo, caption with size control
- Export formats: PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF plus bulk and print-sheet PDF
- History stored in browser localStorage
- Constraint: must remain static and free; no backend or user accounts
- Undecided: formal accessibility standard and brand identity assets

## Evidence on Hand

- Source repository and static site assets in project root
- Live site: https://afnan-samin.github.io/free_qr/
- No formal testimonials, pricing, or licensing claims beyond repository LICENSE

## Product Principles

- Keep creation fast and frictionless
- Preserve privacy by running entirely in the browser
- Keep exports sharp and usable at any size
- Avoid lock-in or required accounts
